Resources

Tools for young artrepreneurs

Free tools, lessons and opportunities at the meeting point of art and business, picked by our team.

01 · Make

Free design tools

Everything you need to start designing without paying for software.

  • CanvaDrag-and-drop design for posts, flyers and presentations, with a free plan.
  • FigmaProfessional design and prototyping tool, free for students and educators.
  • Adobe ExpressQuick graphics, videos and templates with a free plan.
  • CoolorsGenerate and save color palettes for a brand.
  • Google FontsHundreds of free fonts you can use for personal and commercial work.
  • UnsplashFree high-quality photos to use in your designs.

02 · Learn

Learn design

Free places to build your skills, from the basics to branding.

  • Canva Design SchoolFree short courses on design basics, branding and social media.
  • The FuturVideos and lessons on design, branding and the business of creative work.
  • Google Fonts KnowledgeFree guides to choosing and using type well.
  • Smashing MagazineArticles on design and building for the web.

03 · Earn

Business & money basics

The business side of creative work: money, planning and mentorship.

  • Khan Academy: Personal FinanceFree lessons on budgeting, saving, taxes and more.
  • U.S. Small Business AdministrationGuides to planning, launching and managing a small business.
  • SCOREFree mentoring and workshops for small business owners.
  • Junior AchievementPrograms that teach students financial literacy and entrepreneurship.

04 · Grow

Competitions & programs for teens

Places to show your work and test your business ideas. Check each site for current deadlines and eligibility.

  • Scholastic Art & Writing AwardsNational recognition program for creative teens in grades 7–12.
  • Congressional Art CompetitionHigh school art contest; winning work hangs in the U.S. Capitol.
  • DECAStudent organization with competitions in marketing, finance and entrepreneurship.
  • FBLAFuture Business Leaders of America: competitive events and leadership for students.
  • Diamond ChallengeGlobal entrepreneurship competition for high school students.
  • NFTEEntrepreneurship programs and challenges for young people.
Tip from the team: keep every project you make for an AIA client. A small portfolio of real work for real organizations helps with competitions, college applications and your first paid gigs.
