About us

Bridging the gap between art and business

AIA started with two students who loved marketing, graphic design and art, and wanted creative work to get the respect it deserves.

Our story

Why we started

AIA was founded by Emily Tong and Grace Zhou, two business students at Bergen County Academies in New Jersey who bonded over marketing, graphic design and art.

They noticed that art and design were becoming overlooked, especially with the rise of AI, and started AIA to raise the value of creative work and connect it with business and marketing.

Our mission: encourage artistic expression in students, uplift young creative entrepreneurs, and support small businesses in our community.
