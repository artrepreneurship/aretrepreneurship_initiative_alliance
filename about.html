About us

A letter from our founders

Why two business students started a nonprofit about art.

Click the envelope to open it

From the desks of Emily Tong & Grace Zhou
Bergen County, New Jersey

Dear AIA community (and anyone who just found us),

Two business students and a lot of sketchbooks

We met at Bergen County Academies, where we bonded over marketing, graphic design and art. The more we learned about business, the more we noticed that art and design were treated as an afterthought, and with AI tools everywhere, that has only gotten worse.

So we started AIA to show the opposite: good design is worth something, and young artists can bring real value to real businesses.

Today, AIA is more than 60 student members. We design for small businesses and community organizations for free, run workshops on entrepreneurship and financial literacy, and host events that give back. Along the way we've raised over $3,000 and worked with more than 20 organizations. In 2025, we also launched the Bergen County Youth Poet Laureate program.

If you're a student who loves to create, a business that needs a hand, or someone who wants to bring AIA to your own town, there's a place for you here.

Take care,

Emily TongCo-Founder & Co-President
Grace ZhouCo-Founder & Co-President
