Milestones

The AIA story so far

  1. Aug 2024

    AIA opens for business

    Emily Tong and Grace Zhou launch AIA and start taking design commissions from local businesses and student groups.

  2. Aug 2024

    First logo delivered

    A full logo suite for Bing Scientific: a stacked mark and a horizontal wordmark.

  3. Sep 2024

    Chapter applications open

    Students can apply to start AIA chapters, starting with Bergen County Academies.

  4. 2024

    Candid Bronze Seal of Transparency

    AIA earns Candid's Bronze Seal as a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit.

  5. 2024–25

    Client roster grows

    AYLUS Bergen Branch, StrongHER TogetHER, BCA Filipino Club, Intraspectrum Counseling, Global Brigades, Essentia and more.

  6. May 18, 2025

    PPO Picnic booth

    A community booth with spin-the-wheel, a gachapon machine and a table of handmade keychains.

  7. Jun 17, 2025

    AIA Youth Innovation Workshop

    70+ students, a keynote from David Lin and breakouts with Michael Bryzek, Jessica Wang and our own investing team.

  8. 2025

    Bergen County Youth Poet Laureate

    Emily Tong founds the Bergen County YPL, a local chapter of the National Youth Poet Laureate Program.

  9. Sep 2025

    2025–26 chapter applications

    School chapter applications open for the new school year.

  10. Oct 19, 2025

    Design 101 at Bergenfield Library

    A free hands-on workshop for middle schoolers: color, fonts, layout and designing an on-brand Instagram post.

  11. 2026

    Back at the PPO Picnic

    AIA returns to the PPO Picnic for a second year.

  12. Today

    60+ members, 20+ organizations

    $3,000+ raised and 20+ organizations helped with free design.

Featured event · June 17, 2025

AIA Youth Innovation Workshop

“Impact Within Business and Innovation” · 4–6 PM · Bergen County Academies, Hackensack

Our first big in-person event: an afternoon with industry professionals from JPMorgan, Flow Commerce and L’Oréal, hands-on breakout sessions, and a lot of food. We designed everything ourselves, from the posters and banner to the brochures and merch.

70+attendees
3guest speakers
3breakout rooms
$300+fundraising + signups

The speakers

Keynote speaker

David Lin

Founder & CEO of Linvest21; former CTO of JPMorgan’s Global Investment Platform; Columbia Business School alum

Session: Keynote + Q&A on innovation in big companies vs. startups

Breakout: Tech & startups

Michael Bryzek

Co-founder of Flow Commerce and Gilt Groupe (former CTO); MIT graduate

Session: AI in Business

Breakout: Design & branding

Jessica Wang

Marketing Director at L’Oréal and BCA alum (joined virtually)

Session: “Beauty That Sells: What It Really Takes to Build a Brand”

Breakout: Finance & investing

Max & Gevan

AIA student leaders

Session: Investing 101: types of stocks and how investing works

How the afternoon went

  1. 4:00Opening remarks
  2. 4:15Keynote: David Lin + Q&A
  3. 4:45Break: sushi, pizza, cookies + the keychain & merch stand
  4. 5:00Breakout rooms: tech, branding, investing
  5. 5:45Closing, raffle + goodie bags
