Our story

Bridging the gap between art and business

AIA was founded by Emily Tong and Grace Zhou, two business students at Bergen County Academies in New Jersey who bonded over marketing, graphic design, and art.

They noticed that art and design were becoming increasingly overlooked — especially with the rise of AI — and started AIA to elevate the value of creative work and connect it with business and marketing.

Our mission: to encourage artistic expression in students, uplift young creative entrepreneurs, and support small businesses in our community.

Program

Bergen County Youth Poet Laureate

Founded in 2025 by AIA co-founder Emily Tong, the Bergen County YPL is a local chapter of the National Youth Poet Laureate Program, an initiative of Urban Word. It celebrates young poets committed to artistic excellence, civic engagement, and social impact.
